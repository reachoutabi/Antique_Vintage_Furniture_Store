How to Choose Antique & Vintage Furniture

Follow our collector framework to select investment-grade heirloom pieces for your home layout.

1

Identify Era & Style

Determine whether your interior pairs best with 100+ year traditional Antique hardwoods or 20th-century retro Vintage silhouettes.

2

Measure Dimensions

3

Verify Provenance

4

Preservation Knowledge

Care for Your Antique & Vintage Furniture

Essential advice from our master restorers to preserve patina, finishes, and structural integrity for generations.

Cleaning

Dust regularly with a dry, soft lint-free microfiber cloth following the grain. Avoid harsh chemicals.

Wood Care

Apply natural unbleached beeswax twice a year to nourish French polish and original lacquer.

Moisture Protection

Maintain ambient humidity between 45%–55%. Wipe up spills immediately and use coasters.

Sunlight Protection

Position furniture away from direct sunlight or use UV blinds to prevent wood bleaching.

Scratch Prevention

Attach felt pads beneath decorative lamps and heavy objects. Never slide items across polished tops.

Upholstery Care

Vacuum fabrics gently with a soft brush attachment. Condition oxblood leathers with lanolin cream.

Safe Moving

Always lift furniture from the solid base frame rather than dragging or lifting by table tops.

Long-Term Storage

Store in climate-controlled rooms wrapped in breathable cotton dust sheets, avoiding damp cellars.
