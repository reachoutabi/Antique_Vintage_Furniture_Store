Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you guarantee the authenticity of your furniture?

Every piece undergoes rigorous inspection by our senior appraisers, including wood density testing, joinery analysis, and archive cross-referencing. Each item comes with a signed Certificate of Authenticity.

Can I schedule a private viewing at your physical showroom?

Yes, we welcome private viewings by appointment. You can select your preferred date, time slot, and piece of interest via our appointment booking system.

How is international white-glove shipping handled?

We partner exclusively with specialized art and antique logistics couriers. Items are custom-crated, climate-controlled, and insured for 100% of their appraised value during transit.
