Scientific Verification

Museum-Grade Archival Integrity

Every piece entering our Imperial Vault undergoes rigorous three-stage laboratory verification.

Timber & Shellac Micro-Analysis

Dendrochronological tree-ring dating verifies exact felling years of Burmese teak and flame mahogany. Spectrometric analysis checks natural shellac oxidation.

Guild Joinery & Maker Stamps

Hand-cut blind dovetails, iron-forged screws, and stamped cabinetmaker monograms (such as Jules Leleu & Gillows of Lancaster) are cross-verified against guild archives.

Embossed Archival Dossier

Acquisitions include a wax-sealed leather dossier with lineage deeds, ownership provenance, official BADA / LAPADA seals, and lifetime authenticity guarantees.
