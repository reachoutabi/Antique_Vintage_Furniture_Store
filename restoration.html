Guild Craftsmanship

Museum-Grade Furniture Restoration

Combining traditional 18th-century techniques with modern conservation science to preserve historical integrity.

Conservation Philosophy

"Minimum Intervention, Maximum Preservation"

True restoration is not about stripping away age, but honoring the history etched into the timber. Our craftsmen adhere strictly to reversible conservation practices. We never apply synthetic polyurethane varnishes or modern quick-fix adhesives to historical antiques.

Guild Standard Guarantee

Every project includes detailed before/after documentation and a signed conservation certificate.

Our Specializations

Specialized Restoration Disciplines

1. Structural Frame Repair

Consolidation of loose mortise-and-tenon joints using authentic hide glues and period-correct oak/mahogany dowels.

2. Traditional Upholstery

Hand-lashed copper coil springs, stitched horsehair padding, hessian webbing, and hand-rubbed full-grain leathers.

3. French Polishing & Shellac

Hand-applied spirit shellac polishing to restore deep depth of clarity without damaging original timber patina.

4. Veneer & Marquetry Repair

Precision inlay of rare exotic woods (satinwood, rosewood, tulipwood, amboyna burl) matching original grain direction.

5. Ormolu & Hardware Fitting

Cleaning, gilding, and lost-wax casting of missing brass handles, escutcheons, and mercury-gilded bronze mounts.

6. Custom Conservation Projects

Tailored conservation programs for estate collections, historic homes, museums, and private archives.
