Founding Philosophy

Guarding the Craftsmanship of Past Centuries

Established in 1984 by Lord Henry Aethelgard, our gallery began as a modest restoration workshop dedicated to 18th-century English mahogany cabinetmaking. Over four decades, it has evolved into one of Europe's premier destinations for authenticated period furniture.

We believe that genuine antique furniture is not merely a decorative element, but a tangible link to human craftsmanship, artistic movements, and historic provenance.
